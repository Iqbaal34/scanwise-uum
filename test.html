6 / 6 CORE SAFETY STEPS READY FOR REVIEW
STEP 01

Pause Before Acting

Scammers create urgency to stop you from thinking clearly. Do not let threats or deadlines force you into a financial decision.

STEP 02

Reject Unverified Transfer Instructions

Do not transfer money to a so-called secure account based on an unexpected message. Verify the request independently.

STEP 03

Verify Through Official Channels

Open your banking app independently or navigate to the verified official website. Do not use links provided by suspicious messages.

STEP 04

Contact the Bank Directly

Use the official number on your bank card or a verified official website. Never rely on a phone number supplied in the suspicious message.

STEP 05

Protect Sensitive Information

Never disclose your PIN, password, OTP, or online banking credentials in response to an unsolicited message or call.

STEP 06

Report and Respond Quickly

If you transferred money or shared sensitive information, contact your bank immediately through an official channel. Ask about available protective measures and reporting procedures.

CORE PRINCIPLE

URGENCY IS A REASON TO VERIFY, NOT A REASON TO TRANSFER.

Pause. Verify independently. Contact your bank through an official channel.
