STEP-BY-STEP PREVENTION GUIDE

How to Prevent Any
Digital Scams.

06 ACTIONABLE STEPS
STEP 01

Pause Before Acting

Scammers manufacture urgency to stop you from thinking clearly. Never let threats or countdown deadlines force an immediate financial decision.

STEP 02

Reject “Secure Account” Transfers

Never transfer money to a supposed “secure account” solely because an SMS or call tells you to. Legitimate banks do not ask you to move money to protect it.

STEP 03

Verify Independently via Official App

Open your official banking app or website independently. Never tap links or payment instructions embedded inside a suspicious message.

STEP 04

Call Using an Official Number

Contact your bank using the trusted number on the back of your bank card or verified official website—never a phone number provided inside the suspicious SMS.

STEP 05

Protect Passwords, PINs & OTPs

Never share your password, PIN, or one-time verification code (OTP) with anyone who contacts you unexpectedly.

STEP 06

Report & Secure Immediately

If money has already been transferred or sensitive details shared, contact your bank immediately through an official channel to freeze and report the incident.

    SCAM-PREVENTION PROTOCOL · 06 ESSENTIAL HABITS

    Urgency is a reason to verify,
    not a reason to transfer.

    Follow these six practical verification habits whenever you receive an unexpected financial alert, SMS, or call.

    01 · PAUSE

    Pause before acting on urgent messages

    Scammers rely on panic and countdown threats so you act before thinking. Take a breath and step back from any urgent transfer demand.

    02 · REJECT “SECURE ACCOUNTS”

    Never transfer to a “secure account”

    Legitimate banks never ask you to transfer money into a separate “safe” or “secure” account via SMS to protect your balance.

    03 · INDEPENDENT APP CHECK

    Verify through the official banking app

    Open your official banking app or website independently. Never tap links or payment instructions inside a suspicious text message.

    04 · TRUSTED NUMBER

    Call using an independently verified number

    Use the official number printed on the back of your bank card or official website—never call a number provided inside the suspicious SMS.

    05 · GUARD CREDENTIALS

    Never share passwords, PINs, or OTPs

    No genuine bank representative will ever ask you to read out a one-time verification code (OTP), PIN, or password over a call or message.

    06 · ACT FAST IF COMPROMISED

    Report and secure your account immediately

    If money was transferred or credentials shared, contact your bank immediately via an official channel to freeze your account and file a report.
